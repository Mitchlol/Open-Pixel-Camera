I dunno, just use the app. 

You want to film in a somewhat dim place, and you want your poi to be the brightest thing in the scene.

If everything is white, go somewhere darker.

If you poi are over exposed, lower the brightness on them.

If they need to be that bright to outshine your background, decrease the camera brightness (ISO).

If the minimum brightness/iso is still too bright, increase your framerate (shorter frame times absorb less light).

If frames are skipping, lower your resolution or framerate.

If the app doesn't work complain in the discord.
